# Fellaswimming — Design Context for Impeccable

> One-time design context for `/impeccable` and any design-related agent work. Source of truth for brand, audience, and aesthetic direction. The full strategic brief lives in `docs/product-vision.md`; the hi-fi prototype bundle lives in `docs/design/`; this file is the condensed loader.

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary persona — Mama Risa, 38, full-time mom in upper-middle-class Sidoarjo.** Two children (ages 5 and 8). Native Indonesian speaker, follows parenting accounts on Instagram (e.g. @keluargakita), researches thoroughly before any decision for her kids. Comfortable with modern Indonesian apps (Tokopedia, Halodoc, Jago). Phone time is late-evening (after 21:00) on a 360–390px viewport, one-handed scroll.

**Job to be done:** Make a confident, well-informed decision about a swim school for her child within a week, by comparing options through transparent information and educational content rather than DM-ing every school individually.

**Secondary users:**
- **Husband (decision-maker, 8-min validator)** — needs to scan landing on his phone between meetings and approve Risa's recommendation. Needs trust signals (coach, curriculum, pricing) without parenting context.
- **Children 5–12 (beneficiary)** — sit next to mom and look at gallery photos and coach photos. Visual content must be accessible for pre-readers.
- **Fellaswimming non-IT staff (admin operator)** — runs the admin dashboard daily. Not tech-savvy. Needs to triage leads in under 90 seconds each.

### Brand Personality

**Three-word distillation:** Relatable. Encouraging. Joyful.

**Voice:** Friendly Guide — a peer parent who has walked the journey first, sharing insights honestly. Modern, casual, but always respectful of the urgency around child safety. A peer to parents, not a brand talking down to them.

**Address pattern:** Personalized mix.
- Headlines and greetings: "Bunda/Ayah" (warm, inclusive)
- Body and instructions: "kamu" (peer-friendly modern Indonesian)
- Form labels and functional fields: drop the address (neutral, e.g. "Masukkan nomor WhatsApp")

**Emotional goals:** Calm confidence with moments of warmth. Reassurance without urgency. The feeling of "this is the swim school that gets it" — not the feeling of "buy now before it's gone." Tactile, hand-made playfulness via small details (rotated cards, wavy underlines, confetti); never childish or loud.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual mood — Soft Aquatic, Joyful (v2).** Editorial-magazine structure meets warm Sidoarjo parenting feed. Water-themed palette (sky blue, soft turquoise, sandy beige, soft coral, warm sun yellow) on warm off-white background. Rounded humanist typography. Photo-led (real Fellaswimming photos), with playful but disciplined micro-details: chunky 3D button shadows (Duolingo-style press), wave SVG dividers between sections, small card rotations (±1°), hand-drawn squiggle underlines on accent words in headings, sparing confetti dots inside CTA panels, organic blob-radius photo placeholders. Closer to a thoughtful parenting magazine than to Disney-kids or corporate health-tech.

**Reference anchors:**
- **Halodoc** — clean modern Indonesian healthcare. Information clarity, trust-building info architecture, friendly-without-being-clinical tone. Public surfaces should feel as trustworthy as this.
- **Headspace** — calm wellness brand. Soft palette, generous whitespace, approachable typography, restrained illustration. The base layer of restraint our palette and rhythm should preserve.
- **Duolingo** (button + press feedback only) — chunky button bottom-shadow that "presses" on tap is borrowed deliberately to make CTAs feel tactile. Do NOT borrow Duolingo mascots, neon greens, or hard streak/gamification UI.
- **@keluargakita Instagram** — Indonesian parenting voice. Educational tone, warm Bahasa Indonesia, respect for the reader's intelligence. Copy and content tone match this.
- **Delta app by eToro** — polished, data-dense, premium UX. **Admin surfaces** (dashboard, pendaftaran list, CMS) feel as crafted as this — fast, scannable, clearly hierarchical, dark sidebar + warm content area.

**Anti-references — never look like:**
- Generic Sidoarjo swim school Instagram aesthetic (hard-sell captions, "BURUAN!" copy, fire emojis, three-slide promo carousels)
- Cartoon kids-brand visuals (water mascots, comic-style illustrations, neon/rainbow palettes, Disney-bright primaries)
- Clinical healthcare brand (white-coat photography, dense academic serif body text)
- Corporate Indonesian B2B (formal "Kami sangat senang bisa melayani Anda" jargon, stock photos of people in suits)
- Full Duolingo aesthetic (mascot personality, gamified streaks, neon palettes). Borrow only the press-feedback button motion.

**Theme:** Light mode only for MVP. Background is warm sand (`#FAF8F5`), not pure white. Dark mode considered for Phase 2 if visitor analytics justify. Admin sidebar is the one exception: dark navy (`#1A2332`) sidebar against warm-sand main content.

**Typography (committed):**
- **Heading**: **General Sans** (Fontshare) — geometric humanist with personality. Weights 500/600/700. Loaded via `next/font/local` or remote Fontshare URL.
- **Body**: **Nunito** (Google Fonts) — rounded humanist sans, very readable, friendly tone. Weights 400/500/600/700/800.
- **Mono**: **JetBrains Mono** (Google Fonts) — for eyebrow labels, `display_id` rendering, kbd hints, technical micro-copy. Weights 400/500.

Rationale: Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter (initially considered) were rejected per /impeccable banned-font list — too common across AI-generated work, creates monoculture. General Sans and Nunito are NOT on the banned list, fit the Soft Aquatic + Friendly Guide brief, and remain freely available.

**Color palette (committed, OKLCH per Tailwind v4 best practice).** Each brand color has four variants (base / dark / soft / tint) to support the layered card and button system.

- Primary (Sky): `oklch(0.71 0.14 230)` ≈ `#0EA5E9`
  - dark `oklch(0.55 0.16 230)` · soft `oklch(... / 0.10)` · tint `oklch(0.96 0.03 230)`
- Secondary (Turquoise): `oklch(0.73 0.13 184)` ≈ `#14B8A6`
  - dark `oklch(0.55 0.13 184)` · soft `oklch(... / 0.14)` · tint `oklch(0.96 0.03 184)`
- Accent (Coral): `oklch(0.72 0.16 15)` ≈ `#FB7185` — warmth touches
  - dark `oklch(0.56 0.16 15)` · soft `oklch(... / 0.14)` · tint `oklch(0.96 0.03 15)`
- Sun (Warm yellow): `oklch(0.84 0.13 80)` — sparing highlights, star ratings, "Cara Daftar" eyebrow, hero badge
  - dark `oklch(0.55 0.14 80)` · tint `oklch(0.97 0.04 80)`
- Background: `#FAF8F5` (warm sand)
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` · Muted: `#F5F1EC` · Border: `#E7E2DA`
- Foreground: `#1A2332` (warm near-black) · muted `#5C6573` · subtle `#8C95A3`

### Signature Patterns (the v2 design language)

These are the brand's distinctive devices. Use them deliberately — they create the joyful feel that distinguishes this site from generic health-tech.

1. **Chunky 3D buttons.** Primary CTA = solid color + `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 var(--c-primary-dark), 0 8px 16px -6px <colored-glow>`. On `:active`, `translateY(3px)` and reduce the shadow to `0 1px 0 0 var(--c-primary-dark)` — the button "presses." Same pattern for secondary (with `--c-border` as the press-shadow) and accent variants. Sizes `sm` (h-42), default (h-52), `lg` (h-60).
2. **Bottom-color accent on cards.** Cards use `border: 2px solid var(--c-border)` with `border-bottom-width: 4–6px` and the bottom border colored per the card's role (primary / secondary / accent / sun / success). This is the brand's signature elevation device — it replaces colored left-stripes (which remain banned). Use on kelas cards, pelatih cards, step cards, lokasi detail icons.
3. **Subtle card rotations.** Cards in galleries (kelas, pelatih, testimoni, intro stats, step-cards) rotate `±0.4°` to `±1.2°` to feel hand-arranged. Rotation resets to `0deg` on `:hover`. NEVER exceed ±1.5° — beyond that it reads as broken layout.
4. **Wave SVG dividers** between major sections (sand → muted, muted → sand, sand → primary). A single quadratic-bezier path, 56px tall, fills with the next section's background color.
5. **Wavy/squiggle underlines on accent words** in hero headlines. Inline SVG data-URI `<path stroke='%2314b8a6' ...>` positioned below the word; turquoise for primary accent words, coral for accent.
6. **Eyebrow pills.** Above every section heading: small pill with a pulsing colored dot (`animation: pulse 2.4s ease-in-out infinite`), uppercase mono micro-label, surface background, border with `border-bottom-width: 3px` matching the section accent.
7. **Confetti dots** inside the gradient CTA card at the end of landing — 5 sparse colored circles at low opacity. Reserve confetti for the final "Daftar Sekarang" panel only. Do NOT scatter across the page.
8. **Organic blob photo placeholders** in galleries via `border-radius` cocktails like `40% 60% 60% 40% / 50% 40% 60% 50%`. Each gallery item picks one of `blob-a/b/c/d` or `circle`. Hero photo uses an organic rounded-rect like `50% 50% 40% 40% / 30% 30% 50% 50%`.
9. **Spinning dashed rings + floating decoration shapes** at low opacity around hero imagery (`animation: spin 26s linear infinite`). 1 ring + 1–2 bubbles + 1 dot maximum per region.
10. **Functional backdrop blur** is permitted on sticky headers/topbars (`backdrop-filter: saturate(140%) blur(8px)` against `rgba(250,248,245,0.88)`). This is a readability device for the sticky chrome — NOT decorative glassmorphism. Do not use blur as a card effect.
11. **Dashed connector lines** between numbered steps on desktop (3-step "Cara Daftar" + admin breadcrumbs). 3px dashed, primary at 60% opacity.
12. **Mono micro-labels** (JetBrains Mono, uppercase, letter-spaced 0.04–0.12em) for: photo captions in placeholders, `step-card__meta` ("~ 3 menit"), eyebrow text, `display_id`, kbd hints, footer version stamp.

### Design Principles

1. **Information first, action second.** Every page leads with what the parent came to learn, then offers an action. Hero sections that pitch before educating violate this principle.
2. **Mobile is the only assumption.** Design for one-handed late-evening phone scrolling at 360–390px first. Desktop is a bonus surface that uses the extra width for parallel content, not stretched mobile.
3. **Soft and joyful, not sterile and not loud.** Warmth comes from rounded corners, low-opacity warm-tinted shadows, off-white backgrounds, gentle card rotations, and signature press-feedback. Avoid both: (a) the visual coldness of pure white + sharp corners + generic blue drop shadows, and (b) the over-stimulation of neon palettes + bouncy mascots + carnival typography.
4. **Admin is a product surface.** Bu Sari sees the admin daily; visitors see the landing once. Both deserve equal craft. Density is OK in admin (table-first, fast scan, dark navy sidebar + warm content area, fewer rotated flourishes) but materials and motion feel as considered as the public surface.
5. **No fake urgency, ever.** No countdown timers, no "limited slots," no exclamation chains, no fire emojis. The single "⭐ Paling Populer" pill on the Semi-Privat card is allowed — it is descriptive, not urgent. Trust beats urgency.
6. **Performance is a feature.** Slow pages on mobile are anti-content. ISR caching + image weight discipline are non-negotiable. LCP target: under 2.5s on mobile 4G for landing and article detail.
7. **Signature flourishes are seasoning, not the meal.** Each signature device (rotation, confetti, wavy underline, dashed ring) should appear once per section at most. If three flourishes are competing in the same eye-shot, remove two.

### Hard Bans (still in force after v2 update)

These remain off-limits without an explicit override discussion:

- ❌ **Side-stripe borders** (`border-left:` or `border-right:` >1px as a colored accent stripe on cards/lists/callouts/alerts) — single most overused AI design tell. The brand's bottom-color border is the sanctioned alternative; do not flip it sideways.
- ❌ **Gradient text** (`background-clip: text` + gradient background) — top-3 AI design tell.
- ❌ **Decorative glassmorphism** (blur + glow borders as a card or panel effect). Functional backdrop blur on sticky headers/topbars is permitted; cards never use blur.
- ❌ **Default font picks:** Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Fraunces, Newsreader, Lora, Crimson, Playfair, Cormorant, Syne, IBM Plex (any), Space Mono/Grotesk, DM Sans/Serif, Outfit, Instrument Sans/Serif.
- ❌ **Cartoon mascots, comic-style illustrations, water-droplet characters, rainbow/neon gradients, sparkline-as-decoration.**
- ❌ **Hard-sell exclamations** ("Buruan!", "Daftar Sekarang!!!"), countdown timers, fake scarcity ("Sisa 2 slot").
- ❌ **Center-everything layouts** (left-aligned is the brand default; centering reserved for short-form moments: 480px daftar wizard, confirmation panel, login card).
- ❌ **Pure black (`#000`) or pure white (`#fff`)** — always tint toward the brand hue.
- ❌ **Over-rotation.** Card rotation > ±1.5° reads as broken layout, not playful. Stay subtle.
- ❌ **Stacking flourishes.** Don't combine rotation + confetti + wavy underline + spinning ring in the same eye-shot. Pick one device per visual moment.
- ❌ **Stock photography of any kind.** Use Fellaswimming's own photos, or a placeholder block with mono caption (`foto · sesi grup pagi`) until a real photo is ready.
