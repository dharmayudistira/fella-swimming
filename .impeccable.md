# Fellaswimming — Design Context for Impeccable

> One-time design context for `/impeccable` and any design-related agent work. Source of truth for brand, audience, and aesthetic direction. The full strategic brief lives in `docs/product-vision.md`; this file is the condensed loader.

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary persona — Mama Risa, 38, full-time mom in upper-middle-class Sidoarjo.** Two children (ages 5 and 8). Native Indonesian speaker, follows parenting accounts on Instagram (e.g. @keluargakita), researches thoroughly before any decision for her kids. Comfortable with modern Indonesian apps (Tokopedia, Halodoc, Jago). Phone time is late-evening (after 21:00) on a 360–390px viewport, one-handed scroll.

**Job to be done:** Make a confident, well-informed decision about a swim school for her child within a week, by comparing options through transparent information and educational content rather than DM-ing every school individually.

**Secondary users:**
- **Husband (decision-maker, 8-min validator)** — needs to scan landing on his phone between meetings and approve Risa's recommendation. Needs trust signals (coach, curriculum, pricing) without parenting context.
- **Children 5–12 (beneficiary)** — sit next to mom and look at gallery photos and coach photos. Visual content must be accessible for pre-readers.
- **Fellaswimming non-IT staff (admin operator)** — runs the admin dashboard daily. Not tech-savvy. Needs to triage leads in under 90 seconds each.

### Brand Personality

**Three-word distillation:** Relatable. Encouraging. Transparent.

**Voice:** Friendly Guide — a peer parent who has walked the journey first, sharing insights honestly. Modern, casual, but always respectful of the urgency around child safety. A peer to parents, not a brand talking down to them.

**Address pattern:** Personalized mix.
- Headlines and greetings: "Bunda/Ayah" (warm, inclusive)
- Body and instructions: "kamu" (peer-friendly modern Indonesian)
- Form labels and functional fields: drop the address (neutral, e.g. "Masukkan nomor WhatsApp")

**Emotional goals:** Calm confidence. Reassurance without urgency. The feeling of "this is the swim school that gets it" — not the feeling of "buy now before it's gone."

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual mood — Soft Aquatic.** Editorial-magazine structure meets calm wellness palette. Water-themed colors (sky blue, soft turquoise, sandy beige, soft coral accent) on warm off-white backgrounds. Rounded humanist typography. Photo-led (real Fellaswimming photos), with minimal subtle illustration. Closer to Headspace + Notion than to Duolingo or Disney-kids brands.

**Reference anchors:**
- **Halodoc** — clean modern Indonesian healthcare. Information clarity, trust-building info architecture, friendly-without-being-clinical tone. Public surfaces should feel as trustworthy as this.
- **Headspace** — calm wellness brand. Soft palette, generous whitespace, approachable typography, restrained illustration. Visual energy should approach this.
- **@keluargakita Instagram** — Indonesian parenting voice. Educational tone, warm Bahasa Indonesia, respect for the reader's intelligence. Copy and content tone should match this.
- **Delta app by eToro** — polished, data-dense, premium UX. **Admin surfaces** (dashboard, pendaftaran list, CMS) should feel as crafted as this — fast, scannable, clearly hierarchical.

**Anti-references — never look like:**
- Generic Sidoarjo swim school Instagram aesthetic (hard-sell captions, "BURUAN!" copy, fire emojis, three-slide promo carousels)
- Cartoon kids-brand visuals (water mascots, comic-style illustrations, neon/rainbow palettes)
- Clinical healthcare brand (white-coat photography, dense academic serif body text)
- Corporate Indonesian B2B (formal "Kami sangat senang bisa melayani Anda" jargon, stock photos of people in suits)

**Theme:** Light mode only for MVP. Background is warm sand (`#FAF8F5`), not pure white. Dark mode considered for Phase 2 if visitor analytics justify.

**Typography (committed):**
- **Heading**: **General Sans** (Fontshare) — geometric humanist with personality. Loaded via `next/font/local` or remote Fontshare URL.
- **Body**: **Nunito** (Google Fonts) — rounded humanist sans, very readable, friendly tone.
- **Mono**: **JetBrains Mono** (Google Fonts) — for code snippets in articles, display_id rendering, technical labels.

Rationale: Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter (initially considered) were rejected per /impeccable banned-font list — too common across AI-generated work, creates monoculture. General Sans and Nunito are NOT on the banned list, fit the Soft Aquatic + Friendly Guide brief, and remain freely available.

**Color palette (committed, OKLCH per Tailwind v4 best practice):**
- Primary: `oklch(0.71 0.14 230)` (sky blue ~#0EA5E9)
- Secondary: `oklch(0.73 0.13 184)` (soft turquoise ~#14B8A6)
- Accent: `oklch(0.72 0.16 15)` (soft coral ~#FB7185) — used sparingly for warmth touches
- Background: `#FAF8F5` (warm sand)
- Foreground: `#1A2332` (warm near-black, never pure black)
- Full token table in `docs/product-vision.md` § 5 and `docs/prd.md` § 9.

### Design Principles

1. **Information first, action second.** Every page leads with what the parent came to learn, then offers an action. Hero sections that pitch before educating violate this principle.
2. **Mobile is the only assumption.** Design for one-handed late-evening phone scrolling at 360–390px first. Desktop is a bonus surface that uses the extra width for parallel content, not stretched mobile.
3. **Soft, not sterile.** Warmth comes from rounded corners, gentle (low-opacity warm-tinted) shadows, off-white backgrounds, and intentional micro-details. Avoid the visual coldness of pure white + sharp corners + generic blue drop shadows.
4. **Admin is a product surface.** Bu Sari sees the admin daily; visitors see the landing once. Both deserve equal craft. Density is OK in admin (table-first, fast scan, fewer flourishes) but the materials and motion should feel as considered as the public surface.
5. **No fake urgency, ever.** No countdown timers, no "limited slots," no exclamation marks chaining, no fire emojis. Trust beats urgency, and the brand cannot afford to fake either.
6. **Performance is a feature.** Slow pages on mobile are anti-content. ISR caching + image weight discipline are non-negotiable. LCP target: under 2.5s on mobile 4G for landing and article detail.

### Hard Bans (from /impeccable + brand alignment)

These are off-limits without an explicit override discussion:

- ❌ Side-stripe borders on cards/lists/callouts (`border-left:` 3px+ as colored accent stripe) — single most overused AI design tell
- ❌ Gradient text (`background-clip: text` + gradient background) — top-3 AI design tell
- ❌ Glassmorphism (decorative blur + glow borders) — not aligned with Soft Aquatic warmth
- ❌ Default font picks: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Fraunces, Newsreader, Lora, Crimson, Playfair, Cormorant, Syne, IBM Plex (any), Space Mono/Grotesk, DM Sans/Serif, Outfit, Instrument Sans/Serif
- ❌ Sparklines as decoration, cartoon mascots, rainbow gradients, hard-sell exclamations, countdown timers, "Buruan!" copy
- ❌ Center-everything layouts (left-aligned is the brand default; centering reserved for short-form moments)
- ❌ Pure black (`#000`) or pure white (`#fff`) — always tint toward the brand hue
